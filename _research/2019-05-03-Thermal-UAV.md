This project focuses on integrating thermal cameras for enhanced drone vision. The goal of the project is to successfully implement thermal camera vision on a drone with cheap, lightweight [thermal sensors](https://therm-app.com/therm-app/). The effectiveness of thermal vision will be studied to determine environments in which thermal data shines, after which possible applications can be explored. Pictured here is an experiment of thermal camera calibration using a one dimensional calibration object.
