Drivers soon to be replaced by self-driving vehicles, and It is important to keep the communication between the vehicles and the pedestrians uninterrupted. This project aims to design a system to communicate with pedestrians visually and audibly.

![ped_com_1](/assets/images/ped_com/ped_com_1.png)

Currently, we have developed a system that displays messages to pedestrians based on the velocity of the vehicle.

![ped_com_1](/assets/images/ped_com/ped_com_2.jpg)
![ped_com_1](/assets/images/ped_com/ped_com_3.jpg)

We are also working on installing parametric speakers to send messages to pedestrians who are visually impaired.

![ped_com_1](/assets/images/ped_com/ped_com_4.png)
![ped_com_1](/assets/images/ped_com/ped_com_5.jpg)
