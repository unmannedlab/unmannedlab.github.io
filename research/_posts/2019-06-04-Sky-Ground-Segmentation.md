Recent works in this project include sky-ground segmentation and obstacle detection using thermal sensor information only. We have also successfully demonstrated that existing neural networks, like [YOLO](https://pjreddie.com/darknet/yolo/), can be retrained on thermal data and yield good results. Current work is focused on making autonomy possible with thermal vision exclusively. Future work will focus on cross calibration of thermal and visual data and improving autonomy robustness.
